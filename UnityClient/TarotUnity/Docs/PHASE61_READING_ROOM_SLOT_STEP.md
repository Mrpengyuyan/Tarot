# Phase 61 — Reading Room Card Slots & Ritual Step Bar

The reading room was the weakest of the three shipping screens: the three card
positions read as **bright yellow wireframe rectangles**, and the top step bar
(choose spread → write question → draw → flip → reveal) was five static chips with
no sense of progress. Both made the core interaction screen look half-finished next
to the polished menu and result pages. Phase 61 fixes both.

## Recessed glowing sockets

`TarotSocket.png` (the `MP_CardSocket` decal on the cloth) was a double bright-gold
hard outline with corner ticks — a blueprint line. It is regenerated by
`Tools/UiKitGenerator/gen_uikit.py::make_socket()` into a **card slot pressed into
the velvet**: a soft dark recess, a crisper inner-edge shadow for the pressed lip, a
faint warm gold inner glow, and a single soft lit rim instead of the hard outline.
An empty slot now quietly waits rather than shouting, and it vanishes cleanly once a
card lands on it.

The regen is a one-off asset step (same convention as `gen_wax.py`); the material
`MP_CardSocket` is unchanged, only its texture.

## Ritual step indicator

`RitualStepIndicator` (runtime, on `ReadingRoomCanvas`) drives the step bar and the
socket glows off the `ReadingFlowController` state machine:

- **Step highlight** — the chip for the current step reads gold (plate + brighter
  label + a slight scale accent); steps behind it read as completed; steps ahead stay
  dim. State → step mapping: `SpreadSelect→0`, `QuestionInput`/`ReadyToDraw→1`,
  `Shuffling`/`Drawing→2`, `WaitingForFlip→3`, `ResultReady→4`.
- **Socket glow linkage** — each socket gains a warm additive glow quad
  (`MP_SocketGlow_*`, reusing `MP_WarmGlow`). During the draw and flip steps the
  glows for the **selected spread** (one-card vs the three past/present/advice slots)
  light and breathe, inviting the cards; they go dark once the reading is ready.

The chip labels are referenced through `Graphic`, so the same code colours both the
legacy `UI.Text` and the Phase 50 `TMP_Text` chips.

- `Assets/Editor/Phase61ReadingRoomSlotStepBootstrapper.cs` — builds the socket glow
  quads and wires the indicator into the reading room scene.
- `Assets/Editor/Phase61ReadingRoomCaptureBuilder.cs` — captures the reading room at
  several ritual steps for review.
- `Assets/Tests/EditMode/Phase61ReadingRoomSlotStepTests.cs` — guards the state→step
  mapping, the wired chips, and the socket glow linkage.
- `Tools/UiKitGenerator/gen_uikit.py` — `make_socket()` rewritten for the recessed
  glowing slot.
- `Assets/Art/MidnightParlor/Sprites/TarotSocket.png` — regenerated socket texture.
